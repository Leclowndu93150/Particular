# Particular Reforged 1.21.1

## 1.5.7

- **Fix.** Sable ships and boats no longer spam splashes while sailing. Splashes now only trigger when a ship actually drops into the water, and cruising leaves a foam wake instead.
- **Fix.** Cave dust exclude biomes list now actually works. Thanks to @johnhoffm for the report and the fix ([#42](https://github.com/Leclowndu93150/Particular/issues/42), [#54](https://github.com/Leclowndu93150/Particular/pull/54)).
- **Sable splashes (config).** New `enabledEffects.sableSplashes` toggle to turn off ship splashes and wakes.
